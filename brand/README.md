# Cartridge — shared brand

**Cartridge** is our retro-library client family (Windows, Xbox, Roku, and the
phone remote). It works with [RomM](https://romm.app) but is **unofficial and
not affiliated with or endorsed by the RomM project.**

## Naming & RomM's guidelines (read before renaming anything)

RomM's [brand guidelines](https://docs.romm.app/latest/about/brand-guidelines/)
say a third-party app **may not be named "RomM"** and must not imply
endorsement, and their **logo needs Discord approval** before reuse. So:

- The product **name** is **Cartridge** — never "RomM".
- Titles use **"Cartridge — RomM for <platform>"** (e.g. *Cartridge — RomM for
  Xbox*). "RomM" appears only descriptively, for search, never as our name.
- Every listing carries: *"Unofficial; not affiliated with or endorsed by the
  RomM project."*
- The icon is **our own** (`make_cartridge.py` — a game-cartridge silhouette),
  NOT RomM's gamepad mark, so no approval is required.

## The mark

`brand/out/cartridge/master_1024.png`, generated by `make_cartridge.py`: a
cyan-neon game cartridge on the family navy radial ground, with an inset label
(three accent bars) and connector pins. Regenerate + resample:

```bash
python make_cartridge.py
python resample_icon.py out/cartridge/master_1024.png out/cartridge
```

Outputs: MSIX/Store `Square*Logo.png` + `StoreLogo` + `Wide310x150` +
`SplashScreen`, generic `icon_16..512.png`, a multi-res `icon.ico`, and Roku
channel art (`roku_hd_290x218`, `roku_fhd_336x210`, `roku_focus_540x405`).

Propagation targets:
- `RommForDesktop/build/icon.ico`  ← `icon.ico`
- `RommForXbox/{msix,shell/.../}/Assets/*`  ← matching names
- `RommForRoku/images/*`  ← the `roku_*` art + splash
